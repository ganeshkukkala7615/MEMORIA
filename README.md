\# MEMORIA — Decision Intelligence \& Risk Analytics



\## Overview



MEMORIA is a Decision Intelligence and Risk Analytics platform designed to identify high-risk organizational decisions, evaluate assumption reliability, measure financial impact, and analyze decision outcomes.



The project combines:



\- Python

\- Pandas

\- SQLite

\- SQL

\- Streamlit

\- Data Analytics

\- Risk Analytics

\- Decision Intelligence



\---



\## Business Problem



Organizations make thousands of decisions based on assumptions about:



\- Market conditions

\- Customer behavior

\- Demand

\- Technology

\- Resources

\- Costs

\- Operations

\- Timelines



When these assumptions are incorrect, decisions can generate financial losses, operational problems, and failed outcomes.



MEMORIA analyzes historical decision data to answer:



1\. Which decisions carry the highest risk?

2\. Which departments have the greatest risk exposure?

3\. Which decision types are most risky?

4\. Which assumptions fail most frequently?

5\. How do failed assumptions relate to outcomes?

6\. What is the financial impact of high-risk decisions?

7\. Where are the major organizational risk hotspots?
